# Visual Lore Reference

This note holds narrative-facing visual motifs only. The canonical production rules and pipeline are in [[01 MVP/30 Art Direction (MVP)]]. Rendering ownership and typed manifests are in [[04 Engineering/Architecture]].

## Hidzu Corporation-controlled Tokyo

- Civic technology should look useful, maintained, and politely coercive.
- Cameras are integrated into lights, transit structures, identity gates, and service infrastructure rather than presented as fantasy turrets.
- Public screens combine transit information, safety notices, curated sentiment, compliance language, and suppression.
- Repeated identity frames, queue markings, verification tones, and immaculate wayfinding show how control becomes routine.
- Sodium practical light and cold institutional material create tension without turning the city into a black void.
- Technology cyan means an active connected system. Crimson means confirmed danger. Neither is ambient decoration.

## People in the city

- The protagonist, contacts, civilians, and security are modern human beings, not fantasy operatives.
- Clothing communicates expatriate life, mutual aid, service work, public routine, and corporate security.
- Citizens adapt posture, timing, routes, and conversation to being observed.
- George is a private AR presence perceived only by the protagonist. His canonical form is the recovered dark orb with a cyan concentric core and restrained geometric markers; he is not a physical companion or holographic human bust.

## Faction distinction

- **Hidzu Corporation:** bright civic confidence, identity continuity, controlled service, frictionless safety language.
- **Eisenclave/ESD:** American operational integration, harder enforcement, infrastructure capture, and Cold Iron logistics.
- **Shelterline/NARC:** later-campaign improvised continuity, evacuation, analog redundancy, and human networks rather than a generic “resistance neon” palette.

## Campaign motifs

- Missing-person imagery and transfer numbers connect personal absence to institutional logistics.
- Transit, freight, and credentials connect Tokyo escape to Miami and Cold Iron.
- Public optimism and private uncertainty should appear in the same frame whenever possible.
- Humor lives in human improvisation and bureaucratic absurdity, never in supernatural imagery.

## Historical boundary

Earlier depth bands, vector themes, procedural city notes, attack-state actor sheets, tactical overlays, generated clutter, camera-FX defaults, and assetless Level 0 foundations are implementation history. They remain recoverable in Git and the verified GET-139 archive but are not current visual lore or production direction.
