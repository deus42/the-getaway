# Game Design Decision Register

This register records durable product decisions for GET-139. Normative sections 1–12 and 15–16 of canonical system documents contain current Approved rules. Their required sections 13 and 14 may summarize removed behavior and Post-MVP extensions only when clearly labeled non-current. Removed, postponed, and superseded ideas remain fully traceable here so they cannot return through stale code, tests, roadmap text, or tickets.

## Status meanings

- `Approved` — current design contract.
- `Removed` — excluded from the game direction, not merely hidden for Level 0.
- `Postponed` — potentially valid after the vertical slice; not an active promise or dependency.
- `Superseded` — once-current decision replaced by a named newer decision.

The `Supersession relationship` column is directional. Approved/current rows name the older rule they supersede. Removed, Postponed, and Superseded rows say explicitly whether they are `Superseded by`, `Scoped after`, or have no replacement. This prevents a rejected rule from appearing to replace its current successor.
